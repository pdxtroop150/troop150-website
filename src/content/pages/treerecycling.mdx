Let Troop 150 recycle your Christmas tree and wreaths while supporting our
scouts! Request a curbside pickup or bring them to our drop-off lot. Your
donations help support our numerous troop events and outings, while providing
these Scouts with the resources they need to learn leadership skills, build
strong moral character, and give back to the community through volunteer
service.

## Curbside pickup

We will pick up trees the weekend of **{settings.collectionWeekendLabel}**.
Depending on weather and other circumstances, pickup may be delayed. This year, we
are limiting our service area for pickups to the areas of SW and NW Portland
indicated on the map below. Pickup is ${settings.pickupTreeDollars} per tree and ${settings.pickupWreathDollars} per wreath. Pickup
requests must be made by the end of the day on **{settings.requestDeadlineLabel}**.
Please remove all lights, ornaments, tinsel, and stands. No flocked trees.

Bulk pickups outside of our normal service area may be accepted on a
case-by-case basis. Please email
<a href={`mailto:${settings.supportEmail}`}>{settings.supportEmail}</a> to discuss.
