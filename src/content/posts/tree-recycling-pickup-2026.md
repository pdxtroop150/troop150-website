The holidays are full of joy and celebration, but they also come with cleanup duty. Never fear! Troop 150/5150 is here to make it easy and painless for you to recycle your Christmas tree and wreaths in an eco-friendly way while supporting our scouts!

**Curbside pickup:** Request a pickup on our [tree recycling page](/treerecycling) and we'll collect your tree and wreaths from your curb on Saturday, January 9th, 2027. Pickup is $15 per tree and $10 per wreath, and you're welcome to add a donation. The page checks that your address is in our pickup area and takes you straight to secure payment with Square, which accepts Apple Pay, Google Pay, and credit or debit cards. Please request your pickup by Thursday, January 7th.

**Drop-off:** Bring your tree or wreaths to our collection site at St. Andrews Presbyterian Church (3228 SW Sunset Blvd, Portland, OR 97239) between 9am and 4pm any Saturday or Sunday from January 2nd through 17th, 2027. Drop-off is $15 per tree and $10 per wreath, payable at the lot with Venmo, cash, or check. No reservation needed!

Questions, or need a bulk pickup? Email us at [trees@troop150.org](mailto:trees@troop150.org).

Thanks for your support of Troop 150/5150!
